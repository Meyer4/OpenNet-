OpenNet Overview

OpenNet is a small multi-app shell written in Python with Flask. It puts several
self-contained mini applications behind one home screen, so they share navigation,
styling and deployment.

The installed apps are:

  OpenBot    - a chat assistant that answers questions from your documents.
  E-Book     - read and manage e-books.
  AppBox     - a toolbox of small utilities.
  MyFace     - your profile and avatar.
  MyVids     - watch and organise videos.
  Piggy Bank - track savings and spending.
  SnapStyle  - photos, filters and galleries.

Every app is reachable from the home screen at the root URL, and from the Explore
page, which lists each app with a short description. The bottom navigation bar
provides Home, Explore and Settings on every page.

OpenNet is designed to be deployed as a single container or a single process. It
has no database and no build step: installing the Python dependencies and running
the app is enough.
